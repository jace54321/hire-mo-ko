# HireMoKo

Applying for an entry-level job shouldn't require 5 years of experience, a 2x2 ID picture, or fighting with broken Word margins. **HireMoKo** is a single-file, browser-based Harvard resume builder designed for clean, recruiter-approved single-page resumes.

## Features

- **Step-by-Step & Show All Modes**: Guided section-by-section editor with a horizontal stepper rail, or switch to **Show All** to view every section at once.
- **Live Interactive Preview**: Whole-page fit by default (`Fit Page` / `Fit Width`), click-to-select jumping, and double-click inline editing directly on the sheet.
- **Paper Size Support**: Switch between **Short Bond** (`8.5" × 11"` / US Letter) and **A4** (`210 × 297 mm`), with a live 1-page overflow gauge and proper multi-page print margins.
- **Section Templates**: Education, Experience, Projects & Leadership, Skills (tag input), Summary, Bullets Only, Simple List, and Two-Column List.
- **Local-First & Multi-Version**: Everything is stored locally in `localStorage` with multiple resume profiles, Undo/Redo, and JSON export/import.
- **PDF & DOCX Export**: Print-ready vector PDF export (`Ctrl/Cmd+P`) and client-side `.docx` download.
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Monochrome NothingOS-inspired UI with a `DARK | LIGHT` toggle.

## Usage

No build step or backend required:

1. Open https://jace54321.github.io/hire-mo-ko/ in any modern browser.
2. Fill in your details (or click **Load John Doe Sample** to explore).
3. Click **Export PDF** (uncheck *Headers and footers* in the print dialog) or **DOCX**.
